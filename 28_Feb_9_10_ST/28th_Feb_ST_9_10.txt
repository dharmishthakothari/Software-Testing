--Principle of Software Testing 
7 key principles 

1. Testing shows presence of defect 
		by doing testing probability for finding /fixing of defect may be cover but it's doesn't mean product/software is 100% bug free.

2. Early Testing 
		Testing must be start as early as possible.
3. exhastive testing 
		testing each field with each cobmination of data is not possible (m. no could not check with all valid/invalid data)

4. Petiside paradox 
	review and revised the test cases periodically 

5. Defect Clustring 
small no of module may contains more defect then the larger module

6. Tesing is context depend
	baking application and social media context are different to test

7 Absence of error fallcay
	project doen't have any defect but also did not fulfill user requirement .
	it's no use.
